# Salesforce AI Tool — Project Overview

## 1. Project Name

**Salesforce Enterprise AI Tool & Customer Service Assistant**

An enterprise-grade Salesforce AI solution that combines **Salesforce CRM data, AI agents/tools, Generative AI, RAG, automation, and external integrations** to help customer-service users analyze customer information, summarize Cases, recommend actions, retrieve knowledge, and generate context-aware responses.

---

## 2. Executive Summary

The Salesforce AI Tool is designed to demonstrate how modern AI capabilities can be integrated with Salesforce to improve customer-service operations.

The solution provides an AI-powered assistant inside the Salesforce ecosystem. Users can interact with the assistant using natural-language requests such as:

- "Summarize this Case."
- "What is the customer's issue?"
- "Find similar Knowledge Articles."
- "What should be the Case priority?"
- "Draft a response to the customer."
- "Show me the customer's recent Cases."
- "What products does this customer own?"
- "Check the order status."
- "Create a follow-up task."
- "Explain why this Case should be escalated."

The assistant determines the appropriate AI tool, retrieves required Salesforce or external information, applies business rules, optionally retrieves relevant knowledge using RAG, and returns a controlled response.

The project is intentionally designed as an **enterprise portfolio implementation**, demonstrating development, integration, security, AI engineering, testing, DevOps, and production-support capabilities.

# 3. Business Problem

Customer-service representatives often need to work with information distributed across multiple systems.

For example:

```text
Salesforce Case
      |
      +-- Customer Account
      |
      +-- Contact
      |
      +-- Previous Cases
      |
      +-- Knowledge Articles
      |
      +-- Orders
      |
      +-- Product Information
      |
      +-- External CRM / ERP
      |
      +-- Customer Communication
```

Agents may spend significant time:

1. Reading long Case histories.
2. Searching Knowledge Articles.
3. Checking customer information.
4. Reviewing previous Cases.
5. Looking up order information.
6. Determining Case priority.
7. Preparing customer responses.
8. Identifying escalation requirements.
9. Switching between multiple enterprise systems.

The AI Tool addresses these problems by providing a centralized AI-assisted experience.

# 6. Key AI Capabilities

## 6.1 Case Summarization

The AI analyzes Case information and produces a concise summary.

### Input

```text
Case Number: 00012345

Subject:
Unable to process payment

Description:
Customer reports that payment processing fails
after entering card information.

Comments:
Multiple attempts have failed.
Customer contacted support twice.
```

### AI Output

```text
Summary:

The customer is unable to complete a payment transaction.
Multiple payment attempts have failed after card details
are entered. The customer has already contacted support
twice.

Recommended Action:
Investigate payment-processing errors and escalate to
the payment platform team if the issue persists.
```

AI responses should use trusted enterprise information whenever applicable.

```text
Send Email
Change Priority
Escalate Case
Update Customer Data
Create Task
```
